Privacy Policy

Resale Command Center Privacy Policy

Resale Command Center is a desktop inventory, resale-management, market research, shipping-planning, and listing-preparation application.

Effective date September 14, 2026
App Resale Command Center
Platform Desktop application

1. Information The App May Process

The application may process information that the user provides or authorizes, including:

  • inventory/product information
  • purchase and acquisition information
  • package dimensions and weights
  • pricing and market-research information
  • eBay seller/account information made available through authorized eBay APIs
  • eBay listing/draft information
  • OAuth authorization tokens required to maintain an authorized connection
  • application settings

2. eBay Account Access

eBay account access occurs only after the user explicitly authorizes the application through eBay OAuth. Resale Command Center may access eBay information and perform authorized selling/listing operations only within the permissions granted by the user.

The application never needs or stores the user's eBay password. eBay authentication is handled through eBay's authorization system.

3. Credentials And Tokens

API credentials and OAuth tokens used by the desktop application are stored locally using encrypted operating-system-backed storage where available.

Sensitive credentials are not intentionally included in ordinary CSV exports, logs, or reports. Users should never share their API secrets or OAuth tokens with other people.

4. Local Application Data

Inventory and operating data are primarily maintained locally on the user's computer. Local database backups may also exist on the user's computer.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their own computer and backups.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may interact with services selected or authorized by the user, such as eBay, OpenAI, shipping/rate services, and other marketplace or product-research providers that may be added or configured.

Use of those third-party services is also subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. OpenAI / Market Research

If the user enables an OpenAI-powered market-research provider, product-identification information and market-research queries may be sent to OpenAI in order to research products and prices.

eBay credentials, OAuth secrets, passwords, and unrelated private account information are not intentionally sent to OpenAI for market research.

7. Data Sharing

Resale Command Center does not sell personal information.

Information is sent to third-party services only as needed for features the user enables or authorizes.

8. Data Retention / Removal

Local application data remains on the user's computer until the user removes it or uninstalls/deletes the relevant application data.

Disconnecting an eBay account should remove the application's stored seller authorization tokens according to the application's implemented disconnect behavior. Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies.

9. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used, including encrypted credential storage where supported, but no software or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as the application changes. The effective date will be updated when appropriate.
